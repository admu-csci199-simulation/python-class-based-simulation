NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Primary metric: norm_diff = (misinformation_shares / misinformation_count) - (regular_information_shares / regular_info_count)
Positive values indicate greater misinformation sharing relative to availability.
Run-level summaries are the inferential unit to avoid pseudo-replication from within-run agent dependence.
Welch's ANOVA is the primary across-group parametric comparison.
Kruskal-Wallis provides a robust nonparametric confirmation.
Pairwise Welch and Mann-Whitney are practical post-hoc approximations.
